METHOD & CONTEXT

Useful plans show their limits.

Deterministic workflow planning

Tasks wait for dependencies. Quantum tasks additionally wait for one exclusive QPU. The earliest available start wins, with task ID as the tie-breaker. This is list scheduling, not an optimizer.

Up to 1,000 tasks and 50 scenarios. The browser rejects derived integers beyond safe precision; use the Python CLI for larger accounting cases.

Evidence-linked owner assessments

Quality, comparability, requirement support and qualitative risks remain owner judgments. Attached hashes preserve declarations; they do not evaluate the evidence content or establish feasibility.

Independent QBI-aligned research

This workspace supports architecture studies and evidence organization. It is not a QBI performer, a government validation service, or a submission tool. Confirm the applicable QBIT and amendments before any engagement or submission.
