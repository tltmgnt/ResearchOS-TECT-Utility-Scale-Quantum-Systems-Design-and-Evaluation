# Primary sources and QBIT context

Reviewed November 16, 2026. These sources establish research context, not endorsement, eligibility, award or submission status.

- [DARPA Quantum Benchmarking Initiative](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/quantum-benchmarking-initiative): evaluation of pathways to utility-scale quantum computing and independent verification/validation.
- [DARPA QBI 2026, DARPA-PA-26-02](https://www.darpa.mil/work-with-us/opportunities/darpa-pa-26-02): research into fault-tolerant systems and computational workflows containing quantum steps.
- [SAM.gov Stage A QBIT](https://sam.gov/opp/743a55e6e10847b0b8104348a887e4ae/view): example of a topic-specific notice. Check the applicable topic and live amendments for exact scope, requirements and deadlines.

The opportunity description supplied by the project owner states that proposals must respond to a specific QBIT and will not be considered if submitted directly to the parent PA. Confirm the actual topic instructions before any submission. This repository's `qbit` field records references; it does not choose eligibility or submit a proposal.

The scheduling and arithmetic model is defined explicitly in this repository. No example resource factor, timing, quality label, risk label or baseline ratio is attributed to DARPA.
